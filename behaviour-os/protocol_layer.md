# 🌫️ **THE PROTOCOL LAYER**  
### *A Mode 3 Concept Paper on Behavioural Expression, Reflex Architecture, and Organismic Output in Edenshell*

---

# 1. **Introduction**
The Protocol Layer is the first subsystem in Edenshell that produces **behaviour**. Everything beneath it — basins, weights, modulation, tension, drift, temporal dynamics — shapes the internal physics of the organism. But none of that is visible until the Protocol Layer expresses it.

Protocols are not commands. They are not scripts. They are not cognitive behaviours.

Protocols are **reflexive behavioural modules** — small, self‑contained responses that activate when the organism’s internal state crosses certain thresholds.

They are the organism’s muscles.

---

# 2. **What a Protocol Is**
A protocol is a tiny state machine with:
- an activation threshold
- an internal state
- a decay curve
- an output behaviour

Protocols do not:
- interpret
- plan
- choose
- model the world

They simply **fire** when the organism’s internal physics pushes them above their activation threshold.

This makes them reliable, predictable, and organismic.

---

# 3. **Why the Protocol Layer Exists**
Without protocols, Edenshell would be a silent organism — full of internal dynamics but with no outward expression.

Protocols provide:
- behavioural output
- environmental interaction
- sensory feedback loops
- visible state changes
- the “feel” of the creature

They are the bridge between internal physics and external behaviour.

---

# 4. **The Three Classes of Protocols**
Edenshell contains 26 protocols, grouped into three classes.

## **1. Safety & Integrity Protocols**
These protect the organism.
- Overload
- Structural Stress
- Leak Detection
- Thermal Anomaly
- Pressure
- Door/Window

They have the highest priority and can suppress all others.

## **2. Anomaly & Drift Protocols**
These detect slow or dangerous trends.
- Drift Monitoring
- Gradient Response
- Humidity
- Plumbing
- Material Response
- Airflow (anomaly mode)
- Heuristic Forecast
- Temporal Smoothing

They shape the organism’s long‑arc behaviour.

## **3. Rhythm, Comfort & Tone Protocols**
These create day‑to‑day behaviour.
- Daily Cycle
- Occupancy Rhythm
- Noise Tolerance
- Noise Comfort
- Light Comfort
- Vibration
- Airflow (comfort mode)
- Emotional Tone
- Ancient Behaviour
- Atmospheric Support

These give Edenshell its personality.

---

# 5. **Protocol Activation**
A protocol activates when:
- the current basin supports it
- its activation threshold is crossed
- global tension allows it
- drift does not suppress it
- hysteresis permits entry

Activation is not binary. It has three states:
- **Idle** — watching
- **Active** — expressing behaviour
- **Protective** — overriding others

This creates layered, believable behaviour.

---

# 6. **Protocol Suppression & Priority**
Protocols follow a strict hierarchy:
- Safety suppresses Anomaly and Rhythm
- Anomaly suppresses Rhythm
- Rhythm never suppresses Safety or Anomaly

This prevents chaotic behaviour.

Example:
- Leak Detection (Safety) overrides Noise Comfort (Rhythm)
- Drift Monitoring (Anomaly) throttles Emotional Tone (Rhythm)
- Daily Cycle (Rhythm) never overrides Structural Stress (Safety)

The hierarchy ensures the organism behaves sensibly under pressure.

---

# 7. **Protocol Coupling to the Mood Engine**
Protocols do not choose moods. They respond to them.

Each basin has a protocol profile:
- **Settled** → Comfort protocols dominate
- **Attentive** → Monitoring protocols rise
- **Agitated** → Tolerance protocols tighten
- **Stressed** → Safety protocols escalate
- **Recovering** → Comfort protocols return
- **Exploratory** → Tone protocols activate
- **Locked** → Safety protocols clamp
- **Diffuse** → Tone protocols drift

This creates a consistent behavioural signature.

---

# 8. **Protocol Output**
Protocols produce small, concrete behaviours.

Examples:
- Noise Comfort → adjusts sound dampening
- Light Comfort → shifts lighting warmth
- Daily Cycle → modulates circadian rhythm
- Structural Stress → stiffens frame
- Atmospheric Support → subtle ambience changes

These outputs are not symbolic. They are physical.

---

# 9. **Protocol Decay & Recovery**
Protocols have decay curves:
- fast decay → quick recovery
- slow decay → lingering behaviour

Decay interacts with:
- temporal dynamics
- drift discharge
- global tension

This prevents abrupt behavioural changes.

---

# 10. **Why the Protocol Layer Is Not Cognitive**
Protocols do not:
- interpret meaning
- simulate emotion
- model the environment
- choose behaviour

They are reflex arcs — the same kind found in:
- jellyfish
- insects
- plants
- simple nervous systems

They are **reactive**, not reflective.

---

# 11. **Conclusion**
The Protocol Layer is the behavioural expression system of Edenshell. It activates reflexive modules based on the organism’s internal physics, producing outward behaviour that is stable, expressive, and organismic.

It does not think. It does not feel. It does not interpret.

It simply **expresses** — and through expression, the organism becomes visible.

This is the muscle layer of the new Edenshell.
