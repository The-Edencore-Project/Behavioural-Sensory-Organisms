# 🌫️ **THE 8×8 MOOD ENGINE**  
### *A Mode 3 Concept Paper on Basin Adjacency and Organismic Transition Dynamics*

---

# 1. **Introduction**
The Mood Engine is the dynamical core of Edenshell — the system that governs how the organism moves between its eight behavioural basins. These basins (moods) are not emotions or cognitive states. They are **attractor regions** in a complex, reactive landscape shaped by sensors, protocols, and global state.

This paper defines the **8×8 Mood Transition Matrix**, the structural map that determines which transitions are natural, which are rare, and which are impossible. It is the behavioural geography of the organism.

---

# 2. **The Eight Basins (Moods)**
Edenshell’s behavioural space collapses into eight stable basins:

1. **Settled** — low tension, stable behaviour
2. **Attentive** — sensors rising, protocols primed
3. **Agitated** — thresholds crossed, rapid oscillation
4. **Stressed** — sustained pressure, protective behaviour
5. **Recovering** — post‑stress stabilisation
6. **Exploratory** — low threat, high variability
7. **Locked** — one sensor or protocol dominates
8. **Diffuse** — high entropy, drifting behaviour

These basins emerge naturally from the architecture; they are not programmed.

---

# 3. **The Purpose of the 8×8 Matrix**
The 8×8 matrix defines:
- which basins are adjacent
- which transitions are natural
- which transitions are rare
- which transitions are impossible

It is the **structural skeleton** of the Mood Engine.

Without this structure, Edenshell would:
- jump unpredictably between states
- behave chaotically under pressure
- lose organismic coherence

The matrix ensures that behaviour flows like a living system.

# 5. **Interpretation of the Matrix**
Each basin has a unique transition profile.

## **Settled**
- Naturally rises into **Attentive**
- Can drift into **Exploratory**
- Falls into **Recovering** after mild perturbation
- Cannot jump to **Stressed** or **Locked**

## **Attentive**
- Gateway basin
- Moves up to **Agitated** or down to **Settled**
- Can drift into **Exploratory**

## **Agitated**
- Escalates into **Stressed**
- Can collapse into **Locked**
- Can fall back into **Attentive**

## **Stressed**
- Oscillates with **Agitated**
- Collapses into **Locked**
- Recovers into **Recovering**

## **Recovering**
- Returns to **Settled**
- Can relapse into **Stressed**
- Can drift into **Diffuse**

## **Exploratory**
- Drifts into **Diffuse**
- Returns to **Settled**
- Cannot enter **Agitated**, **Stressed**, or **Locked**

## **Locked**
- Can only exit into **Agitated**, **Stressed**, or **Diffuse**
- Cannot return directly to **Settled** or **Attentive**

## **Diffuse**
- High‑entropy basin
- Weakly connected to almost everything
- Often a transitional state
