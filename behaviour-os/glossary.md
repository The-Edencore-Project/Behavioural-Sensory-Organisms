# 🌫️ **BEHAVIOUR‑OS GLOSSARY**  
### *A Mode 3 Canonical Glossary of Terms for the Organismic Behaviour Architecture Underlying Edenshell*

---

# **A**

### **Activation Threshold**  
The internal value a protocol must exceed before it becomes active.  
Determined by basin, tension, drift, and protocol class.

### **Ambient Expression**  
Low‑intensity, continuous output behaviours (lighting, airflow, tone) shaped by the Expression Layer.

### **Ambient Pattern Memory**  
A low‑resolution memory of environmental rhythms used to stabilise long‑arc behaviour.

---

# **B**

### **Basin**  
One of eight stable behavioural modes in the Mood Engine.  
Basins are attractors — the organism “falls” into them.

### **Behaviour‑OS**  
The full organismic behaviour architecture powering Edenshell.  
A non‑cognitive, physics‑based behavioural system.

### **Behavioural Climate**  
The long‑arc behavioural atmosphere created by tension, drift, and temporal dynamics.

---

# **C**

### **Climate (Behavioural)**  
The slow, overarching behavioural tendency shaped by drift and tension.  
Distinct from moment‑to‑moment behaviour.

### **Coupling (Protocol)**  
The interaction between protocols and internal physics (basin, tension, drift).

---

# **D**

### **Decay**  
The gradual reduction of behavioural intensity over time.  
Part of temporal dynamics.

### **Directive Expression**  
Clear, intentional output behaviours (alerts, warnings, protective actions).

### **Drift**  
Long‑arc behavioural bias — the organism’s tide.  
Accumulates from transitions, discharges in stabilising basins.

### **Drift Vector**  
An 8‑dimensional vector representing accumulated bias toward each basin.

---

# **E**

### **Ease‑In / Ease‑Out Curves**  
Expression curves that smooth behavioural transitions.

### **Edenshell**  
The first real‑world implementation of Behaviour‑OS.

### **Expression Layer**  
The outward‑facing organ that translates internal physics into visible behaviour.

---

# **F**

### **Forcing (Sensor)**  
External pressure applied to the behavioural landscape via sensor modulation.

---

# **G**

### **Global Tension**  
The atmospheric pressure system shaping the entire behavioural climate.  
Rises with pressure and volatility; falls with recovery.

### **Gravitational Structure**  
The weighted relationships between basins in the 8×8 matrix.

---

# **H**

### **Hysteresis**  
A stabilising mechanism that prevents rapid oscillation between basins.

---

# **I**

### **Inertia (Behavioural)**  
Resistance to rapid behavioural change.  
Part of temporal dynamics.

### **Internal Physics**  
The combined forces of basins, tension, drift, modulation, and temporal dynamics.

---

# **L**

### **Landscape (Behavioural)**  
The full behavioural topology defined by the 8×8 matrix and its deformations.

### **Locked Basin**  
A high‑tension, high‑inertia basin representing behavioural clampdown.

---

# **M**

### **Matrix (Weighted Mood Engine)**  
The 8×8 transition matrix defining the gravitational pull between basins.

### **Modulation (Sensor‑Modulated Weights)**  
Real‑time deformation of the matrix based on environmental pressure.

### **Momentum**  
Behavioural carry‑over from previous ticks.  
Part of temporal dynamics.

### **Mood Engine**  
The core behavioural landscape containing the eight basins.

---

# **O**

### **Organismic System**  
A system that behaves like a living creature without simulating cognition.

---

# **P**

### **Pressure (Environmental)**  
External forces acting on the behavioural landscape via sensors.

### **Protocol**  
A reflexive behavioural module with activation thresholds, decay, and output behaviour.

### **Protocol Layer**  
The organ that activates reflex behaviours based on internal physics.

---

# **R**

### **Recovery (Behavioural)**  
The downward movement from high‑tension basins toward stability.

### **Reflex Architecture**  
The non‑cognitive behavioural system underlying protocols.

### **Rhythmic Expression**  
Sinusoidal or cyclical output behaviours shaped by temporal dynamics.

---

# **S**

### **Safety Protocols**  
Highest‑priority protocols that protect the organism (e.g., structural stress, leak detection).

### **Sensor Modulation**  
The process of converting raw sensor data into behavioural pressure.

### **Settled Basin**  
The organism’s low‑tension, high‑stability resting state.

### **Stabilisation Phase**  
The part of the Unified Loop that applies inertia, dwell, and decay.

---

# **T**

### **Temporal Dynamics**  
The time‑based forces shaping behaviour: inertia, dwell, decay, momentum.

### **Tension (Global)**  
The scalar value representing behavioural pressure across the entire system.

### **Tick**  
One full cycle of the Unified Behaviour Loop.

---

# **U**

### **Unified Behaviour Loop**  
The circulatory system of Behaviour‑OS — the full behavioural cycle that runs every tick.

---

# **V**

### **Volatility**  
Environmental instability that increases tension and accelerates transitions.

---

# **W**

### **Weighted Mood Engine Matrix**  
The 8×8 matrix defining transition probabilities between basins.

---

# **Conclusion**
This glossary defines the canonical vocabulary of Behaviour‑OS.  
It ensures clarity, consistency, and coherence across the entire field.

Behaviour‑OS is not cognitive.  
It is not emotional.  
It is not interpretive.

It is **organismic physics** — and these are its terms.
