# ⚡ Runtime Loop — Quick Q&A

---

## Q1 — What is the Runtime Loop?
**A:** The heartbeat of the Edencore organism — the cycle that keeps all subsystems coordinated.

---

## Q2 — How often does it run?
**A:** At a fixed interval called the “tick.”  
The exact timing is part of the Identity Layer.

---

## Q3 — What does each phase do?
- Perception → gather data  
- Interpretation → understand meaning  
- Coordination → decide actions  
- Action → move softly  

---

## Q4 — Why can’t subsystems act independently?
**A:** Because independent actions cause noise, vibration, pressure spikes, and discomfort.

---

## Q5 — What makes the loop feel alive?
**A:** Micro‑delays, timing jitter, posture shifts, and soft‑action physics.

---

## Q6 — Does the loop make Edencore conscious?
**A:** No.  
It makes it **coherent**, not conscious.

---

## Q7 — What happens if the loop stops?
**A:** The house becomes a pile of disconnected devices — the organism collapses.
