# ⚡ QUICK Q&A — E‑CORE (Four‑Layer Architecture)

---

## Q1 — What *is* E‑CORE?
**A:** The architectural model that makes Edencore behave like a coordinated organism instead of a pile of IoT devices.

---

## Q2 — What are the four layers?
**A:**  
1️⃣ **Taxonomy** — the organs  
2️⃣ **Behaviour** — the physics of softness  
3️⃣ **Coordination** — the nervous system  
4️⃣ **Identity** — the presence/personality  

---

## Q3 — Why does E‑CORE need four layers?
**A:** Because a living system needs structure (organs), rules (behaviour), control (coordination), and character (identity).  
Without all four, it’s just a smart home.

---

## Q4 — Which layer is the most important?
**A:** The **Coordination Layer** — it’s the runtime loop that makes everything act as one organism.

---

## Q5 — Which layer makes Edencore feel “alive”?
**A:** The **Identity Layer** — timing jitter, micro‑delays, posture, stability windows, privacy membrane.

---

## Q6 — Which layer defines the eight subsystem names?
**A:** The **Taxonomy Layer** (HVAC, LUMA, SAC, ENVO, APLO, MEDI, MOTA, NEMO).

---

## Q7 — Which layer controls softness and smoothness?
**A:** The **Behaviour Layer** — all the ramping, staggering, smoothing, and anti‑vibration rules.

---

## Q8 — Does E‑CORE replace smart‑home platforms?
**A:** No.  
It sits *above* them — it’s the organism model, not the protocol.

---

## Q9 — Why does E‑CORE feel more realistic than typical sci‑fi AI?
**A:** Because it’s grounded in physics, timing, airflow, pressure, noise, and human comfort — not magic.

---

## Q10 — What’s the next step after defining the four layers?
**A:** Building the **Coordination Layer** (the runtime loop) so the organism actually “thinks.”
