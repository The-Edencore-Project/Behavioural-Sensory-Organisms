# 2. Interpretation Layer  
This is where the heatmap becomes meaningful.

## 2.1 CALM 😌  
The organism is relaxed.  
Most subsystems sit at **medium** or **low** intensity.  
Only ENVO and SAC maintain moderate vigilance.

**Signature:**  
Soft, stable, minimal movement.

---

## 2.2 ALERT 🚨  
This is the highest‑intensity posture.  
SAC, ENVO, and HVAC spike to **maximum**.  
LUMA and MOTA increase sharply.  
APLO stays controlled but responsive.

**Signature:**  
The organism becomes sharply aware without becoming chaotic.

---

## 2.3 NIGHT 🌙  
The quietest posture.  
HVAC, LUMA, APLO, MEDI, MOTA all drop to **low**.  
SAC and ENVO remain moderately active for safety.

**Signature:**  
Ultra‑soft, whisper‑quiet, minimal disturbance.

---

## 2.4 RECOVERY 🔧  
This is the stabilisation posture.  
HVAC and ENVO dominate to restore the envelope.  
APLO and MOTA are suppressed.  
NEMO re‑syncs external conditions.

**Signature:**  
The organism “re‑centres” itself.

---

## 2.5 TRANSIT 🚶  
Movement‑oriented posture.  
LUMA and MOTA spike to **maximum**.  
HVAC and ENVO increase to smooth airflow and predict movement.  
APLO suppresses mechanical cycles.

**Signature:**  
Guidance, smoothing, path‑lighting, motion‑friendly behaviour.
