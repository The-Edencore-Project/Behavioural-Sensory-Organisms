# 🧩 E‑CORE — The Four‑Layer Architecture  
## Edencore Coordinated Organism Runtime Environment  
### Mode 2 — System Overview

E‑CORE is not a single mechanism.  
It is a **stack of four interlocking layers**, each responsible for a different dimension of the house‑organism.  
Together, they create the physical, behavioural, and perceptual identity of Edencore.

---

# 1️⃣ The Taxonomy Layer — *“The Organs”*  
### (E‑CORE‑8 Subsystem Classification)

This layer defines **what the organism is made of**.  
It is the anatomical map — the eight subsystems that form the body.

- **HVAC** — lungs & circulatory air system  
- **LUMA** — eyes & ambient perception  
- **SAC** — skin & perimeter awareness  
- **ENVO** — nerves & sensory input  
- **APLO** — muscles & mechanical action  
- **MEDI** — voice & communication  
- **MOTA** — limbs & mobility aids  
- **NEMO** — external senses & neighbourhood context  

📌 *This layer answers:*  
**“What are the parts of the organism?”**

---

# 2️⃣ The Behaviour Layer — *“The Physics of Softness”*  
### (How each subsystem must behave)

This layer defines the **rules of motion**, the physical style of Edencore.  
It ensures the house feels alive, gentle, and coordinated.

Key behavioural rules:

- **Soft‑start everything**  
- **Ramp, don’t snap**  
- **Stagger mechanical loads**  
- **Avoid vibration stacking**  
- **Smooth pressure transitions**  
- **Dim before brighten**  
- **Quiet before loud**  
- **Predict discomfort before it happens**  

📌 *This layer answers:*  
**“How should the organism move?”**

---

# 3️⃣ The Coordination Layer — *“The Nervous System”*  
### (The Runtime Loop)

This is the **engine** of E‑CORE — the part that continuously evaluates the environment and orchestrates the subsystems.

It processes:

- ENVO sensor data  
- SAC movement & occupancy  
- HVAC load & pressure  
- APLO mechanical cycles  
- LUMA brightness levels  
- MEDI soundscape  
- MOTA accessibility needs  
- NEMO external conditions  

It resolves conflicts, predicts discomfort, and synchronises actions.

📌 *This layer answers:*  
**“How does the organism think?”**

---

# 4️⃣ The Identity Layer — *“The Presence”*  
### (The subtle behaviours that make Edencore feel alive)

This is the layer most systems never have.  
It’s the **behavioural personality** of the house — not emotional, not sentient, but *present*.

Identity components:

- **Timing jitter** (avoids robotic precision)  
- **Micro‑delays** (feels natural, not mechanical)  
- **Behavioural posture** (calm, alert, night‑mode, recovery‑mode)  
- **Stability windows** (periods of intentional stillness)  
- **Privacy membrane** (what the house chooses not to react to)  
- **Dominance engine** (which subsystem takes priority in conflict)  

📌 *This layer answers:*  
**“What does the organism feel like?”**

---

# 🧠 How the Four Layers Interlock

Think of E‑CORE like a living creature:

- **Taxonomy** = the organs  
- **Behaviour** = the physics of movement  
- **Coordination** = the nervous system  
- **Identity** = the personality  

Each layer depends on the one below it:

1. You can’t coordinate what you haven’t classified.  
2. You can’t define identity without behaviour.  
3. You can’t create behaviour without coordination.  

This is why E‑CORE feels coherent — it’s built like a biological system, not a tech stack.
