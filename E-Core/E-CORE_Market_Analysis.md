## E‑CORE Market Analysis  
### “How Many Homes Have All Eight IoT Categories?”

---

# 1. Introduction  
E‑CORE defines eight IoT subsystems required for a home to behave as a coordinated organism:

- HVAC  
- LUMA  
- SAC  
- ENVO  
- APLO  
- MEDI  
- MOTA  
- NEMO  

This paper examines how many real‑world homes currently possess *all eight* categories, and what this means for Edencore’s positioning in the market.

Spoiler: the number is effectively **zero**.

---

# 2. Market Reality: Fragmented Adoption  
Smart‑home adoption is not holistic.  
It is **clustered** around a few popular categories:

- smart speakers  
- smart lighting  
- smart security  
- smart thermostats  

These clusters do not combine into full‑stack ecosystems.  
They form **islands**, not organisms.

### Key data points (UK/US):
- 42% of UK homes have *at least one* smart device  
- 18% qualify as “smart homes” (3+ devices)  
- 63% of US homes have at least one smart device  
- Smart thermostats: ~22–28% adoption  
- Smart lighting: ~38% adoption  
- Smart security: ~33–38% adoption  
- Smart appliances: low adoption, inconsistent across brands  
- Mobility/assistance devices: <1%  
- Neighbourhood mesh systems: 0% (not a consumer category)

No dataset shows homes with all eight categories.

---

# 3. Category‑by‑Category Adoption Analysis  
Below is the E‑CORE‑8 mapped against real‑world adoption.

## 3.1 HVAC — Moderate Adoption  
Smart thermostats exist, but HVAC integration is region‑dependent.  
UK adoption is lower due to heating system diversity.

## 3.2 LUMA — Moderate Adoption  
Smart bulbs and switches are common, but rarely whole‑home.

## 3.3 SAC — Moderate Adoption  
Security is a major driver of smart‑home purchases.  
But full perimeter integration is rare.

## 3.4 ENVO — Low Adoption  
Environmental sensors exist, but usually as isolated devices.  
Few homes have a full sensor suite.

## 3.5 APLO — Low Adoption  
Smart appliances are expensive and inconsistent.  
Most homes have *zero* smart appliances.

## 3.6 MEDI — High Adoption  
Smart speakers and TVs are widespread.  
This is the strongest category.

## 3.7 MOTA — Near‑Zero Adoption  
Automated blinds, accessibility systems, and mobility aids are niche.  
This category is almost nonexistent in consumer markets.

## 3.8 NEMO — Zero Adoption  
Neighbourhood mesh is not a consumer concept.  
No smart‑home platform offers it.

---

# 4. The Statistical Conclusion  
Based on all available market data:

### **Homes with all eight E‑CORE categories: ~0–1% globally.**

Even high‑end smart homes typically reach **3–5 categories**, not eight.

The missing categories are always the same:

- ENVO (full sensor suite)  
- APLO (smart appliances)  
- MOTA (mobility systems)  
- NEMO (external mesh)  

These are the structural gaps in the current market.
