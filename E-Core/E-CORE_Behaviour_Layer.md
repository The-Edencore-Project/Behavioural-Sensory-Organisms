# 🌬️ MODE 3 — Concept Paper  
## E‑CORE Behaviour Layer  
### “Soft‑Action Physics — How the Organism Moves”

---

# 1. Introduction  
The Behaviour Layer defines the **physics of motion** inside the Edencore organism.  
It is not about *what* the house does — it is about **how** it does it.  
Soft‑Action Physics ensures every movement is:

- smooth  
- predictable  
- quiet  
- coordinated  
- physically comfortable  

This is the layer that prevents the house from feeling robotic or mechanical.

---

# 2. The Four Laws of Soft‑Action Physics  

## 2.1 Law 1 — **Ramp, Don’t Snap**  
No subsystem may change state instantly unless safety requires it.  
All transitions must be:

- ramped  
- eased  
- interpolated  

This applies to airflow, light, sound, motion, and mechanical cycles.

---

## 2.2 Law 2 — **Stagger, Don’t Stack**  
Mechanical loads must never activate simultaneously.  
APLO, HVAC, and MOTA actions must be offset to avoid:

- vibration stacking  
- pressure spikes  
- noise bursts  

The house must feel like it breathes, not jolts.

---

## 2.3 Law 3 — **Smooth, Don’t Spike**  
Any action that affects:

- pressure  
- airflow  
- brightness  
- sound  
- vibration  

must be smoothed over time.  
Spikes are forbidden.

---

## 2.4 Law 4 — **Anticipate, Don’t React**  
The Behaviour Layer predicts discomfort before it occurs:

- pre‑warming  
- pre‑cooling  
- pre‑lighting  
- pre‑venting  
- pre‑damping  

The house moves *before* the occupant notices the need.

---

# 3. Behavioural Postures  
The organism has multiple behavioural modes:

- **Calm** — minimal movement  
- **Alert** — heightened SAC/ENVO sensitivity  
- **Night‑Mode** — ultra‑soft actions  
- **Recovery‑Mode** — stabilisation after disturbance  
- **Transit‑Mode** — guiding movement through the house  

Each posture modifies the physics.

---

# 4. Why Soft‑Action Physics Matters  
It creates the Edencore signature:

- no jolts  
- no shocks  
- no flicker  
- no sudden noise  
- no mechanical aggression  

The house feels alive because it moves like a living system.
