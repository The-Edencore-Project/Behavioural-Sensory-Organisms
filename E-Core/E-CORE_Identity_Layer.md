# 🫥 MODE 3 — Concept Paper  
## E‑CORE Identity Layer  
### “The Presence — How the Organism Feels”

---

# 1. Introduction  
The Identity Layer is the **subtle behavioural signature** of Edencore.  
It is not personality.  
It is not emotion.  
It is not AI.  
It is **presence** — the sense that the house is aware, stable, and coordinated.

---

# 2. Components of Identity  

## 2.1 Timing Jitter  
Actions avoid perfect mechanical precision.  
A 200ms micro‑delay makes the house feel natural, not robotic.

---

## 2.2 Micro‑Delays  
Subsystems wait tiny intervals before acting, creating the illusion of thought.

---

## 2.3 Behavioural Posture  
The house shifts between:

- calm  
- alert  
- night  
- recovery  
- transit  

Each posture changes the softness, timing, and sensitivity.

---

## 2.4 Stability Windows  
Periods where the house intentionally does nothing.  
Stillness is part of identity.

---

## 2.5 Privacy Membrane  
The house chooses what *not* to react to:

- background noise  
- distant movement  
- irrelevant fluctuations  

This prevents hyperactivity.

---

## 2.6 Dominance Engine  
When subsystems conflict, one takes priority.  
This creates a sense of intention.

---

# 3. Why Identity Matters  
Without this layer, Edencore would feel:

- mechanical  
- twitchy  
- over‑reactive  
- robotic  

Identity gives the organism **character** without giving it consciousness.
