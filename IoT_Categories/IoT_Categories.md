# IoT Categories — Edencore System Overview  
## (Core Taxonomy for the Harrow House)

This document defines the eight functional categories of IoT systems inside a modern home, and how Edencore interacts with each. These categories form the physical, observable backbone of Edencore’s behaviour. No mystique. No abstraction. Just real systems doing real things.

---

## 01 — HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning)
**Role:** The house’s lungs and circulatory system.  
**Why it matters:** HVAC is the strongest physical influence on comfort, noise, vibration, and environmental stability.

**Components:**
- Heating system (boiler, heat pump, radiators)
- Ventilation (ducts, vents, airflow routing)
- Air conditioning (compressors, fans)
- Humidity control
- Pressure balancing

**Edencore Behaviours:**
- Soft‑start fans to reduce vibration  
- Smooth pressure transitions to prevent floor thuds  
- Stabilise temperature for recovery or sleep  
- Reduce turbulence to quiet the house  
- Delay HVAC events when someone is walking nearby  

---

## 02 — Lighting Systems
**Role:** The house’s eyes and emotional tone.  
**Why it matters:** Lighting is the most visible behavioural signal to humans.

**Components:**
- Smart bulbs and dimmers  
- Ambient strips  
- Motion‑activated lighting  
- Pathway lighting  
- Exterior security lights  

**Edencore Behaviours:**
- Soft, gradual brightness changes  
- Guiding light paths during night movement  
- Reduced glare for injured or sensitive occupants  
- Lighting shifts that match household activity  

---

## 03 — Security & Access Control
**Role:** The house’s perimeter awareness.  
**Why it matters:** Determines occupancy, movement, and safety context.

**Components:**
- Door/window sensors  
- Smart locks  
- Cameras and doorbells  
- Driveway motion sensors  
- Presence detection  

**Edencore Behaviours:**
- Adjusts internal behaviour based on who’s home  
- Prepares rooms ahead of returning occupants  
- Coordinates lighting and HVAC with entry events  

---

## 04 — Environmental Sensors
**Role:** The house’s skin.  
**Why it matters:** Provides raw data that Edencore reacts to.

**Components:**
- Temperature and humidity sensors  
- CO₂ and air quality monitors  
- Vibration sensors  
- Sound level meters  
- Pressure sensors  

**Edencore Behaviours:**
- Learns resonant frequencies  
- Detects unusual vibration patterns  
- Adjusts airflow based on CO₂ levels  
- Softens the house when someone is unwell or injured  

---

## 05 — Appliances & Utilities
**Role:** The house’s muscles.  
**Why it matters:** These create noise, vibration, and timing conflicts.

**Components:**
- Washing machine, dryer, dishwasher  
- Fridge compressor  
- Boiler ignition  
- Water pumps  
- Heat pump cycles  

**Edencore Behaviours:**
- Staggers appliance cycles  
- Prevents simultaneous vibration sources  
- Pauses loud appliances when someone is moving  
- Soft‑starts motors to reduce thumps  

---

## 06 — Media & Communication Devices
**Role:** The house’s voice and ears.  
**Why it matters:** Controls soundscape and information flow.

**Components:**
- Smart speakers  
- TVs  
- Intercoms  
- Tablets and phones  
- Notification systems  

**Edencore Behaviours:**
- Adjusts volume based on room occupancy  
- Pauses media when someone leaves  
- Reduces audio harshness during recovery periods  

---

## 07 — Mobility & Assistance Devices
**Role:** The house’s hands.  
**Why it matters:** Supports accessibility, recovery, and movement.

**Components:**
- Smart blinds and curtains  
- Automated doors  
- Adjustable beds  
- Stair lights  
- Lift chairs and mobility aids  

**Edencore Behaviours:**
- Slows door movement for injured occupants  
- Activates path lighting for night mobility  
- Adjusts blinds to reduce glare or heat  

---

## 08 — Neighbourhood Mesh
**Role:** The house’s nervous system.  
**Why it matters:** Provides context beyond the walls.

**Components:**
- Shared environmental signals  
- Shared timing patterns  
- Local weather and pressure data  
- Community noise and activity levels  

**Edencore Behaviours:**
- Pre‑adjusts for incoming storms  
- Syncs quiet periods with neighbourhood patterns  
- Adapts airflow based on external pressure shifts  

---

## Summary
These eight categories form the complete IoT landscape of the Harrow House. Edencore’s intelligence emerges not from emotion or personality, but from the coordination of these systems into a single, coherent behavioural engine. Every action is physical, observable, and grounded in real‑world mechanics.
