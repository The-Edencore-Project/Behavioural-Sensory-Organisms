# Edencore IoT Category Abbreviations  
## (Shortlist for System Taxonomy)

This list defines the standard abbreviations for all IoT categories used within the Edencore architecture. These abbreviations appear in documentation, logs, behaviour maps, and story references.

---

## 01 — HVAC  
**Heating, Ventilation & Air Conditioning**  
Primary environmental control system.

---

## 02 — LGT  
**Lighting Systems**  
All interior and exterior illumination devices.

---

## 03 — SAC  
**Security & Access Control**  
Perimeter sensors, locks, cameras, presence detection.

---

## 04 — ENS  
**Environmental Sensors**  
Temperature, humidity, CO₂, vibration, sound, pressure.

---

## 05 — APU  
**Appliances & Utilities**  
Washing machine, dryer, dishwasher, fridge, boiler, pumps.

---

## 06 — MCD  
**Media & Communication Devices**  
Speakers, TVs, intercoms, tablets, notification systems.

---

## 07 — MAD  
**Mobility & Assistance Devices**  
Blinds, curtains, automated doors, stair lights, lift aids.

---

## 08 — NMS  
**Neighbourhood Mesh System**  
Shared environmental and timing signals across nearby homes.

---

## Summary
These abbreviations form the backbone of Edencore’s technical language.  
They allow clean referencing, consistent naming, and fast categorisation across all system behaviours, logs, and story elements.
